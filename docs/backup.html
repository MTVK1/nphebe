Back up your data

Create a database backup before major updates, computer changes, or whenever your notes are important.

1

Open Nphebe

Sign in and open the application's settings area

2

Choose database export

Select the option for downloading, exporting up your database.

3

Choose a safe location

Save the exported DB file somewhere you control, such as an external drive or secure storage.

4

Keep the backup

Do not delete your previous backup until you have verified that the new backup is available and usable.

Good backup practice

  • Keep more than one backup for important data.
  • Store backups separately from the computer running Nphebe.
  • Do not publish DB files on public websites or repositories.
  • Make a fresh backup before major software updates.
