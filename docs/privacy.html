Nphebe Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nphebe handles information when you use the Nphebe desktop application.

1. Overview

Nphebe is a desktop notes application designed around local use. Nphebe does not use Google authentication. Accounts are handled through Nphebe's local authentication system.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Nphebe may store information you enter into the application, such as your email/account information, notes, and other application data. This information is stored as part of the application's local data.

3. Your notes and application data

Nphebe does not claim ownership of the notes or other content you create. You remain responsible for the content you enter and for maintaining backups of important information.

4. Online collection

Nphebe is not designed around a subscription cloud service. Unless a particular release explicitly states otherwise, Nphebe does not intentionally upload your notes to a Nphebe-operated cloud database.

5. Local database backups

Nphebe allows you to export/download a database file and import/restore a database file. These files may contain your application data. You are responsible for protecting backup files and choosing secure storage locations.

6. Authentication

Nphebe uses local authentication. Your account credentials are used to access the local application experience. Nphebe does not require a Google account or Google OAuth for sign-in.

7. Third-party services

Nphebe does not require Google authentication. If a future version integrates a third-party service, the applicable documentation or privacy notice will identify that service before or when it is used.

8. Security

We aim to provide reasonable safeguards for the application, but no software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your computer, account credentials, and exported database files.

9. Children's privacy

Nphebe is not specifically directed at children. Users should comply with applicable age and privacy requirements in their jurisdiction.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nphebe's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published with a new effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, use the official Nphebe contact/support channel provided with the current release or website.
